# 04-01 PLAN: Anti-Slop Industrial Hero, Asymmetric Architecture & Studio Showcase

## Objective
Redesign and elevate the Amonite CAD landing page (`src/app/page.tsx`) and navigation (`src/components/ui/navbar.tsx`) to production-grade product design standards, strictly following the **ANTI-SLOP FRONTEND DESIGN & TASTE ENGINE** directives.

## Design Constraints & Explicit Ban List Enforcements
1. **No Startup Gradients & Background Glows**:
   - Remove `from-cyan-400 via-sky-300 to-blue-500` text gradients and `shadow-[0_0_25px_rgba(6,182,212,...)]` radial card glows.
   - Use calibrated deep graphite (`#08090c`), dark zinc tone surfaces (`#101218`, `#161922`), precision bone white (`#f3f4f8`), and cold steel accents.
2. **No Three-Column Identical Feature Cards**:
   - Replace the 3-column feature row with an asymmetric bento specification grid:
     - Wide primary tile: Real-time 3D Solid Kernel with live viewport telemetry (coordinates, render mode, FPS, active memory).
     - Tall secondary tile: Client-Side Vector & Mesh Interchange Matrix (Binary STL 50-byte record breakdown, AutoCAD R12 DXF entity parser, SVG extrusion).
     - Asymmetric third tile: Zero-Egress Invariant & Session Persistence (client-only mathematical pipeline, zero cookies, zero network telemetry).
3. **No Centred Symmetry**:
   - Hero is strictly structured as an asymmetric split: Left-anchored typographic block with offset industrial telemetry, juxtaposed against a precision right-anchored WebGL canvas.
4. **No Heavy Visual Borders**:
   - Transition from harsh dark borders (`border-[#232938]`, `border-[#1f2636]`) to subtle hairline dividers (`border-white/[0.06]`), structural whitespace, and tonal depth.
5. **No Lazy Text Layouts & Placeholder Copy**:
   - Write authentic, rigorous CAD/CAM engineering copy detailing raycasting, polygon triangulation, and tolerance metrics.

## Implementation Tasks
1. **Audit & Refactor `src/components/ui/navbar.tsx`**:
   - Monochromatic industrial brand badge with official ammonite spiral.
   - Streamlined, clean technical actions: "Launch Studio Sandbox", "Architecture Docs", and minimal waitlist trigger.
2. **Re-architect `src/app/page.tsx`**:
   - Asymmetric two-column hero with live interactive CAD canvas and telemetry status indicators.
   - Asymmetric bento grid for technical specifications.
   - Offset technical CTA section replacing generic banner cards.
   - Precision footer with direct engineering contact (`amoniteadmin@gmail.com`).
